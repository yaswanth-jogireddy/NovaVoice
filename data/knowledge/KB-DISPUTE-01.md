# Disputing an unrecognised transaction

Raise a dispute as soon as you notice an unrecognised debit. The assistant creates a dispute request and, for a card transaction, offers to block the card. Disputes are reviewed within 10 working days; a provisional credit may be given while the review is open. Reporting within 3 days of the transaction gives the strongest protection.
