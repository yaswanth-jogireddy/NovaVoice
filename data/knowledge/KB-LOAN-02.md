# Prepayment and foreclosure

Floating-rate home loans for individuals have no prepayment charges. Fixed-rate and personal loans may carry a foreclosure charge of up to 4 percent of the prepaid amount. A foreclosure statement can be requested and is emailed within 2 working days.
