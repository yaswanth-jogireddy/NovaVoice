# How the voice assistant verifies you

To protect you, the assistant verifies your identity before discussing any account: your customer ID or registered mobile, and your date of birth. Sensitive actions such as blocking a card or raising a dispute also need a one-time code sent to your registered mobile. After three failed attempts the call is transferred to an officer.
