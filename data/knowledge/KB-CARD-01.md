# Lost or stolen debit card

Report a lost or stolen card immediately. After identity verification and a one-time code, the voice assistant blocks the card instantly. Blocking is permanent; a replacement card is dispatched to the registered address in 5-7 working days at no charge for the first replacement in a year. Pending card transactions already authorised may still post.
