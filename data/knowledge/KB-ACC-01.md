# Opening a new savings or current account

A new savings or current account can be opened in the Nova Trust app with video KYC in about 15 minutes, or at any branch. You need your PAN card and Aadhaar (or a passport) and a mobile number linked to Aadhaar. The voice assistant cannot open an account on a call, but it can raise a request for the accounts team to call you back and guide you through it. If you forgot your customer ID for an existing account, verify with your registered mobile number instead.
