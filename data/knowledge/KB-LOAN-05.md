# Personal, vehicle and home loan products

Nova Trust Bank offers personal loans from 50,000 to 25 lakh rupees for 12 to 60 months, vehicle loans for new and used cars and two-wheelers for up to 84 months, and home loans for up to 30 years. Interest rates depend on the applicant's profile and are confirmed only in the sanction letter.

To apply you need identity proof, address proof, the last 3 months' salary slips or 2 years' income tax returns for self-employed applicants, and the last 6 months' bank statements. Vehicle loans also need the vehicle quotation; home loans need the property documents.

You can apply in the Nova Trust app, on the website or at any branch. Repayments are monthly EMIs debited on the due date from your Nova Trust account.
